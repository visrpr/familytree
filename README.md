# 🌳 Prabhu Family Tree

A living, growing record of our family history.

## Visit the site

**[Open the Family Tree →](https://visrpr.github.io/familytree/)**

## What you'll find

- **Interactive Family Tree** — explore generations and branches visually.
- **Biographical Profiles** — dates, milestones, and stories for each member.
- **Photo Gallery** — a growing archive of family photographs.
- **Timeline & Statistics** — see the family's story unfold over the years.
- **Relationship Check** — find out how any two members are related.

## Contribute

This tree is meant to be expanded forever. Anyone can help:

- **Add family members** — spouses, children, siblings, or parents.
- **Add photos** — upload and arrange pictures for a loved one.
- **Update details** — correct or fill in names and important years.

Every contribution is saved permanently and shared with everyone who visits. Questions or ideas? We would love to hear them.
